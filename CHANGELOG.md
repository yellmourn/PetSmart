# PetSmart Classic Changelog

## v1.15.9 (Beta) - Initial Classic Era Release

PetSmart Classic is the WoW Classic Era version of PetSmart, a Hunter pet-care and pet-reference addon. It keeps your pet fed and happy, and shows where to find, tame and train pets, with all of its data set up for Classic Era.

### Pet Feeding
- A movable, resizable icon (the Feed Pet icon) opens a food panel showing only the food in your bags that your current pet's family will eat.
- Click any food in the panel to feed it to your pet.
- Food is sorted into Classic's six categories: Bread, Cheese, Fish, Fruit, Fungus and Meat.

### Compendium
A separate reference window with a section icon for each part:
- **Current Pet:** while a pet is out, shows its name, level, family, loyalty and experience, its stats, and its learned and unlearned abilities.
- **Abilities Index:** every Classic pet ability and rank, with pet level, training point cost, trainer cost, and the creatures that teach it when tamed. A paw beside each ability shows whether it's in your Beast Training yet: green if it is, red if not.
- **Pet Index:** all 17 Classic pet families, each with its combat role, stat modifiers, diet and abilities, and every creature you can tame for that family. A silver paw marks rares and a bronze paw marks teacher pets. Click a creature for its own page.
- **Rare Index:** the 66 rare tameable pets, with a section listing the rares in your current zone.
- **General Training Info:** loyalty, pet food and happiness, training points, taming, abilities, stable masters and pet command modes.
- **Options:** profiles, tooltips, the minimap button, map pins and more.

### 3D Model Viewer
- Rare, teacher pet and creature pages show the creature in 3D. Drag to turn it, right-drag to move it, use the mouse wheel to zoom, and double-click to reset the view.

### Tracking
- Track rares and teacher pets on the world map and minimap, with highlighted spawn areas for many teacher pets.
- Track one at a time, or turn on Multi-Tracking in Options to track several.

### Pet Experience Bar
- A movable, resizable bar showing your pet's name, level and experience. Options are under Options > Pet Experience Bar.

### Other
- Per-character profiles: switch, copy, reset or delete.
- Minimap button (LibDBIcon) and a LibDataBroker launcher for Titan Panel, Bazooka and similar addons.
- Slash commands: `/petsmart` or `/ps` shows or hides the Compendium, and `/petsmart options` opens Options.
